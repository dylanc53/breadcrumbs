Breadcrumbs Support

Breadcrumbs is a door-to-door canvassing map for sales teams. Email dylancraig53@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Getting started

Joining your team
Create an account, choose "Join a team," and enter the six-character join code your manager sends you.
Starting a team
Choose "Start a team" when you sign up. You become the manager and can invite reps with your team's join code, found under the account menu.
Logging a visit
Tap a house on the map (or use the Pin button for your current location), mark it Cold, Warm, or Hot, add a note, and save.
Recording your route
Tap "Start selling" before you begin knocking. Your walking route draws itself on the map, and continues recording with your phone in your pocket. Tap Stop when you're done for the day.

Common questions

My route stopped recording partway through.
Open your device Settings, find Breadcrumbs, and set Location to "Always" with Precise Location on. Without "Always," iOS stops recording when the app leaves the screen.
Can my teammates read my notes?
No. Teammates see where you have visited and your route trails, but your lead statuses, notes, and customer details are visible only to you and your team's managers.
The address on my pin is wrong.
Addresses come from the nearest known address point, which can pick a neighbor on large lots. Drag the pin before saving to correct it.
The voice note button isn't working.
Allow both Microphone and Speech Recognition for Breadcrumbs in your device settings.
