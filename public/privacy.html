Breadcrumbs Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Breadcrumbs is a door-to-door canvassing tool for sales teams. Reps log the houses they visit and their walking routes; their team and managers use that record to coordinate coverage. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who can see it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a password (stored only as a secure hash), plus the team you belong to and your role on it.
  • Location — your device's GPS position. Precise location is recorded only while you have an active selling session running (between tapping "Start selling" and "Stop"). On iOS this includes location collected while the app is in the background or the screen is off, so your route is complete. No location is collected when a session is not running.
  • Visit records you create — the coordinates and street address of houses you pin, the lead status you assign, your notes, and any customer contact details you choose to enter (name, phone, email).

How the information is used

  • To draw your visits and walking routes on your team's map.
  • To show teammates where your team has already covered, and to share your live position with your team while a selling session is active.
  • To give managers reporting on their team's activity.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone outside your team and the service providers listed below.

Who can see your data

  • You see everything you have logged.
  • Teammates see where you have visited and your route trails, but not your lead statuses, notes, or customer contact details.
  • Managers of your team see all of their team's records, including notes and customer details.
  • Nobody outside your team can see your team's data. Teams are isolated from one another at the database level.

Information about third parties

If you enter a customer's name, phone number, or email into a visit note, you are recording information about another person. Only enter details you are permitted to collect, and follow your employer's policies and applicable law when doing so. Contact us at the address below to have such records removed.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and hosting of your account and visit data.
  • Mapbox — map imagery and converting coordinates to street addresses. Coordinates are sent to Mapbox for this purpose.
  • Vercel — hosting of the web application.
  • Apple — distribution of the iOS app, including TestFlight when used.

Retention and deletion

Visit and route records are kept for as long as your team uses the service, because they are your team's operating history. Live location entries are deleted as soon as a selling session ends. If a member is removed from a team, their login is deleted while their historical visit records remain with the team, no longer attributed to a named account. To delete your account and associated data, email the address below and we will action the request within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Location tracking runs only during selling sessions you start yourself. You can end a session at any time with the Stop button.
  • You can revoke the app's location or microphone permission at any time in your device settings. The app still works; route recording and voice notes will not.
  • Notes and customer details are optional fields.

Children

Breadcrumbs is a workplace tool and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify team managers.
